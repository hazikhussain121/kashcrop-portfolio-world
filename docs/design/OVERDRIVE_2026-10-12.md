## Direction contract
THESIS: The work is a moving opening title. A spatial reel of actual project screens is the first interaction; readable full case studies reward the next click.

OWN-WORLD: Cobalt #2448E8, white #F7F8FF, citron #F5EB6A, pale blue #E9EEFF. Sora for language and Barlow Condensed for monumental brand lettering. Precise media frames, open typography and broad saturated surfaces. No decorative micro-labels, fake metrics or simulated application controls.

STORY: Visitors recognise product design, engineering and applied AI through real projects, explore a case, understand delivery and support, and reach the existing enquiry flow.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact wordmark and navigation above a wide typographic statement and an interactive spatial project reel. Actual interfaces dominate the stage. Project switching and a case-study action stay visible; a large KashCrop wordmark anchors the composition. On phones, a compact headline sits above a touchable reel with ordinary controls.

FORM: Motion-title design studio, candidate 5. Seed 35e932bf, supported-network rerun confirmed assignment through API. Signature interaction: drag or step through the project reel, with damped perspective and a complete reduced-motion fallback.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Challenger evaluation
Variable-font specimen is competitive on audience identification but loses product clarity when type eclipses the work. Raise: lettering receives specimen-grade optical spacing and responsive sizing.
Blue Note session sleeve is competitive on visual composition but loses clarity for institutional project buyers. Raise: one disciplined colour field and image rhythm carry the entire experience.
Cutting-bench select rail is declined on audience identification and clarity. Raise: the project reel exposes its actual selection and next action with ordinary controls.
Iridescent cloud edge is declined because atmospheric material cannot evidence software. Raise: depth should come from real interface surfaces and light, never empty decoration.
Pickling calendar is declined because its state grammar would misrepresent a portfolio. Raise: real project metadata is concise and legible.
Gothic Lolita mook is declined because it adds unrelated cultural signals. Raise: every route receives the same typographic and navigation craft.
