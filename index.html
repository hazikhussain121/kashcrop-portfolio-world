02 / Work

Our projects include institutional websites, diagnostic tools, research software and mobile apps.

    We build software around the work.Vizier assists the people doing it.

    Vizier uses an organisation’s own data and documents to answer questions and support specific tasks.

    See our projectsStart a project
    01 — Unify

    It unifies your scattered data.

    Documents, records, images, past cases. Vizier pulls them into one retrievable source of truth.

    02 — Reason

    It retrieves, then reasons.

    Retrieval-augmented: every answer is grounded in your own data, and it cites where each one came from.

    03 — Act

    It answers in your shape.

    Structured output tailored to your workflow, and the next action taken inside your tools.

    04 — Improve

    It sharpens with every use.

    Every correction and new case is folded back in. Vizier learns your domain deeper over time, so it grows more accurate and more relevant the longer it runs.
