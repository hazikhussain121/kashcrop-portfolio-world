# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Founders, institutional leaders, and agricultural experts in Kashmir and beyond who need practical digital systems. They care about actual workflows, field conditions including low connectivity, multilingual use, clear language, and reliable operation after delivery.

## Product Purpose

Show KashCrop Innovations' real portfolio, explain its connected approach to product design, engineering, and applied AI, and capture project enquiries. Visitors should quickly understand what the studio builds, see the work clearly, and find a direct next step.

## Brand Personality

Grounded, expert, confident, and direct. Let the products demonstrate the quality of the work. Keep copy concise, specific, and rooted in what has actually been built. Creative ambition should help people understand the studio's capabilities and make the work memorable.

## Design Principles

- Lead with the work. Show real product interfaces at a useful scale.
- Make exploration easy. Keep projects, services, supporting details, and contact routes clear.
- Keep the complete story. Project pages retain capabilities, scope, technologies, references, and source context.
- Give motion a purpose. The spatial reel and limited media movement support exploration while preserving a complete static experience.
- Keep claims grounded. Historical captures and synthetic demonstrations describe interface work; they do not establish current release status, model accuracy, adoption, or laboratory results.

## Evidence and Voice

Use actual project captures where they exist. Where a case uses synthetic interface demonstrations, identify that material and retain its dates, captions and provenance. Do not imply that demonstration data is a live customer workflow or measured result.

Avoid unrelated imagery, fabricated project metrics and fragmented visual identities. The portfolio should demonstrate both design judgement and engineering capability. Keep technical details where they help a buyer assess a case or scope; keep primary navigation and calls to action direct.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast requirements and provide visible keyboard focus, readable text, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support. Honor the system preference and the site's motion control.

Preserve accessible dialog behavior, ordinary project links, searchable URL state, screen identifiers, useful empty/error states, and readable scrolling for long interface captures. Reel exploration must work through buttons and keyboard as well as drag. Optional recordings use manual controls and pause when their disclosure closes.

Preserve the existing contact delivery and validation workflow. Do not send a live test enquiry merely to verify styling.
