# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are prospective clients, institutional partners, research teams, and people evaluating KashCrop Innovations from outside Kashmir. They arrive with limited time and need to understand what the company actually builds, see credible proof, and decide whether to start a conversation.

## Product Purpose

This is the public replacement for kashcrop.in: a company-first portfolio for KashCrop Innovations. It should make the company, its software/product work, its applied-AI practice, and its founder legible quickly, then reward deeper exploration with real project evidence and a clear contact path.

## Positioning

KashCrop builds web, mobile, and AI systems around real operational work, from idea to launch. Vizier is the company’s reusable evidence-grounded software layer: it works with an organisation’s own records and documents to support specific tasks.

## Operating Context

Visitors may be on a laptop during a business or institutional review, or on a phone from a shared link. The site is evaluated alongside other studio, product, and founder portfolios. It must communicate from the first viewport without requiring visitors to understand the interaction model first.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site is a static Vite web app suitable for Cloudflare Pages.
- The requested surface is an explorable, side-scrolling portfolio world inspired by the feeling of a 2D journey, with projects represented as places encountered along the route.
- The interaction must remain a portfolio: project information, evidence, links, contact, and a conventional accessible path cannot be hidden behind play.
- Existing verified case-study content, company identity, SKIIE incubation attribution, founder identity, and external proof links are the source of truth.
- Do not invent customers, metrics, endorsements, funding, capabilities, or project outcomes.
- Desktop and portrait mobile are first-class targets; touch, keyboard, reduced motion, and low-power fallbacks are required.

## Brand Commitments

- KashCrop Innovations is the company boundary; KashCrop is one product, not the whole company.
- Vizier is a named product/system and must be described precisely.
- Preserve the real company name, Srinagar/J&K location, founder identity, SKIIE/SKUAST-K incubation attribution, and existing project evidence.
- The voice should be restrained, concrete, and human rather than promotional or game-like in its copy.

## Evidence on Hand

- Live incumbent: https://kashcrop.in/
- Local source evidence: `src/content.js`, `public/founder.jpg`, `public/founder-wide.jpg`, `public/skiie-logo.png`, and project proof links.
- Existing source checkout used as content and asset reference: `C:\PROJECTS\vizier-web`.
- No new game artwork or custom sound pack has been commissioned yet; first build should use authored vector/CSS/canvas scenery plus existing verified imagery where it earns its place.

## Product Principles

1. Make the company legible before asking visitors to explore.
2. Let real work provide the spectacle; interaction should reveal evidence, not obscure it.
3. Preserve a direct, low-friction route to every project and to contact.
4. Keep cultural and institutional references specific and accurate.
5. Make the experience resilient: readable without motion, touch-friendly on portrait screens, and fast enough to load as a public site.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic project links, readable contrast, reduced-motion behavior, screen-reader access to the project index, touch targets appropriate for portrait mobile, and a non-canvas/low-motion fallback that exposes the same content and actions.
